Methodology

ADX — Average Directional Index

ADX measures the strength of a trend — not its direction. It is derived from the smoothed difference between the +DI (upward directional movement) and −DI (downward directional movement) relative to the Average True Range over 14 periods.

ADX = SMA₁₄( |+DI − −DI| / (+DI + −DI) × 100 )
ADX RangeTrend Strength
0 – 25Weak / No Trend
25 – 50Strong
50 – 75Very Strong
75 – 100Extreme

A high ADX with a Neutral signal means the market is moving with energy but technical indicators are split on direction — a common early-trend or reversal scenario.

Buy / Sell Signal — Technical Indicators

The aggregate signal is a consensus vote across 16 technical indicators. Each indicator casts a Buy, Neutral or Sell vote. The majority outcome determines the overall signal.

CategoryIndicators
OscillatorsRSI, STOCH %K, CCI, Momentum, MACD, Williams %R
Moving AveragesEMA 10/20/30/50/100/200, SMA 10/20/30/50/100/200
Signal = majority(Buy votes, Neutral votes, Sell votes) Strong Buy → ≥ 65% of votes are Buy Strong Sell → ≥ 65% of votes are Sell

The signal reflects the current snapshot at the selected timeframe. Higher timeframes (1h, D) produce more reliable signals than 1m or 5m.
