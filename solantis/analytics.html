RSI Divergence Scanner

Every divergence,
explained on the chart.

27 instruments, four timeframes, one scan. Each hit comes with its own chart, so you see exactly why it was flagged — and when it was confirmed.

  • First RSI low — the RSI turns up, lower than the 5 candles on each side.
  • Second, higher RSI low — price makes a lower low, the RSI doesn't. Selling pressure is fading.
  • Divergence drawn — price and RSI lines point in opposite directions.
  • Confirmed — five more candles close. The signal can no longer repaint.
